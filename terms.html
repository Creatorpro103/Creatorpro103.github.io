Using this site

By using this site, placing an order, or sending a form, you agree to these terms. If that is not okay with you, please don't use the site.

Orders

Everything is 3D printed and made to order, so colors and finish can look a little different from the pictures and from print to print. That's just how 3D printing is.

Please double check your details before you submit. We'll email you to confirm, and an order isn't final until we do. Sometimes we have to turn an order down, like when something is out of stock or can't be printed, and if that happens we'll let you know.

Prices and payment

Prices are in US dollars and can change, but a confirmed order stays at the price it was confirmed at.

You pay us directly after ordering, using the options on the form (Venmo, Zelle, or cash when ready, depending on the item). We make your order once payment is sorted out. We will never ask for a card number, bank login, or password on this site.

Shipping

A lot of orders are handed off locally. If you want yours shipped, put your address on the form. Shipping costs extra, and the amount is listed on the page for that item.

Delivery times are estimates. Prints take time, and preorders go out when they're ready. Once a package is with the post office, delays are out of our hands, but reach out and we'll help however we can.

Preorders

A preorder holds your spot for something that isn't ready yet. We'll try to hit any date shown on the page, but those dates are goals, not promises.

Safety

Keychains have small parts and aren't toys, so they're not for kids under 3. Printed plastic also isn't made to hold heavy weight, so please don't rely on one for anything that matters.

Reviews, replies, and messages

We check reviews and replies before they go on the testimonials page. If you post one, it should be honest, in your own words, and something you're allowed to share. You're also letting us show it on the site.

Please keep it kind and real. No spam, no harassing anyone, and no sharing other people's private info. We can edit or remove anything that doesn't fit.

Spam protection

The checkout, contact, preorder, and testimonials forms use Google reCAPTCHA to keep bots out. It collects things like your IP address and browser details so Google can tell people from bots. Google's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service apply to it. Please don't try to get around it.

Your information

We only collect what you type into a form: your name, email, order details, how you plan to pay, an address if you want shipping, and any message or review you write. We use it to make and deliver your order, email you about it, answer questions, and run the testimonials page.

That information is kept in Google tools like Sheets and Gmail. We don't sell it. The site also uses a couple of small cookies, like remembering that you closed a notice or limiting how often a review can be posted. You can clear them in your browser whenever you like.

Designs and content

The site, the iPrint Keychains name, and our original models belong to us, so please don't copy or resell them. If you ask for a custom design, only send artwork you have the right to use. We may say no to anything that copies someone else's characters, logos, or brands.

Changes
